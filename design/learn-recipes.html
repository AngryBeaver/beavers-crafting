3 · A blueprint, not known yet: "Learn" top right

Visible to whoever may see it (Foundry permissions, as today): greyed out with a lock, "Not known", Start disabled. "Learn" is top right, for players too. The GM (or everyone, with the setting "Players learn directly") learns at once.

3b · A player clicked "Learn": waiting for the Gamemaster

Setting "Gamemaster approves" (default): "Waiting for approval" instead of the button. It stays after closing the window or reloading (the request is kept on the actor), so the same request can't be sent twice.

4 · The Gamemaster decides, the player gets a popup

The request is a chat card whispered to the Gamemasters only: the player doesn't see it. Allow learns it (same rules: no duplicates, preLearn hook), Reject ends the request. The player gets no chat message, only a popup, or at the next login when offline. No Gamemaster online: the card is posted when one comes.

5 · The setting

How players learn a blueprint with "Learn" in the crafting window. The Gamemaster always learns directly.

Rules behind the mockup

  • Known = a recipe item on the actor (as today).
  • Who sees a world recipe: Foundry permissions (as today). Who can craft it: the kind under the name, Recipe (default, so existing worlds don't change) or Blueprint (must be learned; flags.beavers-crafting.recipe.kind). Book: later, as another kind.
  • Learn: drag & drop, the "Learn" button (top right), or api.learn(actor, uuid). All of them: no duplicates (same source), hook beavers-crafting.preLearn (return false refuses, with a message).
  • Players: setting "Learning recipes" = Gamemaster approves (default): the request is kept on the actor (flags.beavers-crafting.learnRequests), the active Gamemaster gets a whispered chat card (Allow / Reject), the player a popup with the answer. Players learn directly: like the Gamemaster.
  • Follow the source: a learned recipe keeps _stats.compendiumSource and is shown and crafted from the current source; a recipe changed on the actor stays local.
  • Forget: button for the actor's owner, top right, acts at once (no confirmation); hook beavers-crafting.forget after the recipe is removed.
  • Compendium recipes stay unlisted (as today); learn them by drag & drop or the API.
  • One setting: "Learning recipes" (Gamemaster approves | Players learn directly).
